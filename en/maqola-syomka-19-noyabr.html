Filming at Makon: videos about the Tourism and Travel Agency Course

On 19 November, videos for Madina Central Asia's Instagram page were filmed at Makon. They give information about the Tourism and Travel Agency Course, which takes place at Makon.

The filming took place right inside Makon, among the workspaces. The presenter is Zilola. Makon's interior is in the shots too: desks with computers, armchairs and a terracotta wall.

About the course

The Tourism and Travel Agency Course is run by the Madina Central Asia travel agency; classes take place at Makon. It is for beginners and for people already working in tourism.

What the course covers:

  • Hands-on tourism classes
  • Anti-crisis strategy and marketing
  • Targeted advertising
  • Islamic principles of trade

Practice. You work in real tour operators' booking systems. The student book and workbook are given in print, and you receive a certificate at the end.

In short: 13 lessons, 15 topics · 8 people per group · 3 times a week · price $450 (not included in Makon membership, paid separately).

More about the course and sign-up — in the course section on the home page.

Where to watch the videos

The videos are posted on Madina Central Asia's Instagram page: @madina_central_asia
