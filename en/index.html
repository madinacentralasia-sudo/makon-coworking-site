What is Makon?

Makon is a place where ideas are born. Here, amid the smell of coffee and the pages of books, big things begin. You build your own work, meet driven people and grow together.

Inside Makon

What's inside

  • A free personal lockerNo need to carry your things back and forth every day.

Ask a question

Where is Makon?

11 Lashkarbegi Street, Tashkent, in the Akai City residential complex. A 2-minute walk from Hamid Olimjon metro station, with parking.

What are the opening hours?

Every day except Sunday, from 10:00 to 19:00.

How much is membership?

For Makon residents — $250 a month, for travel agents — $350 a month.

What's included?

Your own dedicated desk, Wi-Fi, a free printer, a free personal locker and books for residents.

What else does the travel agent plan include?

Everything residents get, plus a licence to operate, financial, legal and organisational services, and ready client requests. You work from Makon without opening an office.

Who is Makon for?

For people who have their own work and want to grow: students, targeting specialists, freelancers, entrepreneurs and travel agents.

How do I pay?

By QR code (UzQR) only, in your banking app. Cash is not accepted.
