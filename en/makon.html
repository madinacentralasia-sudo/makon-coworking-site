What is Makon?

Makon is a place where ideas are born. Here, amid the smell of coffee and the pages of books, big things begin. You build your own work, meet driven people and grow together.

Inside Makon

What's inside

  • A calm settingNo noise and no strangers — your focus stays on your work.
  • Your own dedicated deskNo hunting for a seat every day: your desk is waiting for you.
  • Wi-Fi and a free printerEverything you need for work is at hand.
  • A free personal lockerNo need to carry your things back and forth every day.
  • Coffee and booksFor a break and for meeting new people.
Around Makon

What's around

  • A prestigious addressIn the Akai City premium residential complex in the city centre, next to Premium Avenue. You can invite a client or partner with confidence.
  • 2 minutes to the metroJust a 2-minute walk from Hamid Olimjon station — you arrive on time every day, whatever the traffic.
  • Convenient parkingIf you come by car, you won't waste time looking for a spot.
  • Everything close byA boutique, a flower shop, a restaurant and shops a few steps away: lunch, a meeting or a gift — it's all nearby.
